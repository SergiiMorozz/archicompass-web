## Article 02 - Prepare an Interior Design Brief

## Article 04 - Interior Project Timeline

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_04_Harmonogram_projektu_wnetrza_PL_EN.docx`

## Release order

1. Article 01: establishes the core project-planning cornerstone.
2. Article 02: connects the first guide to the product's structured brief.
3. Article 03 and Article 04: build the renovation-planning cluster.
4. Article 05: starts the style and inspiration cluster.

Each release package must be prepared in Polish and English together. The
entire launch remains deliberately non-indexable until all 15 topics have
passed the same review.
