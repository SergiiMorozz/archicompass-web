## Editorial standards applied to all five guides

- Polish and English are independent, complete reader-facing versions rather
  than machine translations or shortened summaries.
- Every package contains a key takeaway, structured sections, one responsive
  comparison table, eight localized FAQs, metadata, focus keywords, cover ALT
  text and contextual internal links.
- Claims remain practical and evidence-conscious. No artificial market data,
  rankings, guarantees or universal price promises are used.
- Links lead to the relevant ArchiCompass guide cluster, AI Project Compass or
  Designer Directory. Links to still-protected guides are intentional internal
  launch links and will resolve before the full collection is published.

## Article 06 - Choose an Interior Designer

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_06_Jak_wybrac_projektanta_wnetrz_PL_EN.docx`

The published package turns the original criteria into a comparison framework:
scope, portfolio context, process, communication, written terms and red flags.
It helps readers use the same brief for several conversations, rather than
choosing on images or price alone.

## Article 07 - Architect or Interior Designer

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_07_Architekt_czy_projektant_wnetrz_PL_EN.docx`

The package explains where the roles commonly differ and where they overlap.
It intentionally avoids legal conclusions: structural changes, formal matters
and technical responsibility must be confirmed with the appropriately qualified
professional and, where relevant, building administration.

## Article 08 - Interior Design Costs in Poland

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_08_Ile_kosztuje_projekt_wnetrza_PL_EN.docx`

The guide explains how to read a proposal by scope, deliverables and support.
It does not present a historic price range as a current market fact. Readers
are directed to compare written proposals for the same brief, property and
service scope.

## Article 09 - Apartment Renovation Budget

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_09_Budzet_remontu_mieszkania_PL_EN.docx`

The guide separates planning, works, finishes, furnishing, logistics and
contingency. It does not prescribe a universal contingency percentage because
the appropriate reserve depends on the existing property, scope uncertainty and
timing of decisions.

## Article 10 - Save Money on a Renovation

**Source:** `ArchiCompass_Article_10_Jak_oszczedzic_na_remoncie_PL_EN.docx`

The guide focuses on avoiding late changes and comparing alternatives with the
same function. It distinguishes prudent value decisions from unsafe reductions
in electrical work, plumbing, waterproofing, substrates and other difficult-to-
repair elements.

## Release dependency

Articles 06-10 are editorially complete but deliberately not public. The full
launch will publish the 15 guides together with final sitemap, structured data,
canonical, hreflang and indexing checks.
