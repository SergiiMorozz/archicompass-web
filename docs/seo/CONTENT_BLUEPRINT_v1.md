# ArchiCompass Content Blueprint v1

**Publication unit:** one complete Polish guide plus one complete English guide  
**Primary audience:** people planning a residential interior project in Poland  
**Launch quantity:** 15 topics / 30 public URLs  
**Indexation:** after every topic below is complete, not before

## Editorial route convention

| Locale | Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polish | `/guides/<polish-slug>` | `/guides/jak-zaplanowac-projekt-wnetrza` |
| English | `/en/guides/<english-slug>` | `/en/guides/how-to-plan-an-interior-design-project` |

The two versions answer the same reader need, but are written naturally for the language. The English page is not a literal or partial translation.

## Standard guide structure

1. SEO title and meta description.
2. H1 and executive summary.
3. Table of contents where the guide has more than three sections.
4. Context, definitions and the decision being made.
5. Practical steps, table, checklist or comparison.
6. Examples and trade-offs, clearly marked as examples.
7. Common mistakes and how to avoid them.
8. FAQ with 8-10 genuine questions.
9. Contextual internal links and a relevant product CTA.
10. Review date and editorial attribution when the process is ready.

## First article: editorial acceptance criteria

`Article_01_Jak_zaplanowac_projekt_wnetrza_ArchiCompass.docx` is a useful source draft, not yet a complete launch guide. Before publication it needs:

- a standalone native English article, not a short outline;
- an executive summary and table of contents;
- a full process section with practical decision points;
- one or more original tables or checklists;
- 8-10 complete FAQs;
- verified internal links to related guides, AI Project Compass and the directory;
- cover and inline images with language-specific ALT text;
- final metadata, open-graph preview and schema validation;
- human editorial review of all factual and commercial wording.

## Quality guardrails

- At least one visual must materially help the reader understand the guide.
- Images of interiors must match the topic; do not use unrelated exterior images for an interior-planning guide.
- Currency and price context must be explicit: what is included, for which scope and whether values are examples rather than market facts.
- Avoid prescriptive legal, tax, structural or safety advice; point readers to qualified professionals where that advice is needed.
- Published content needs both languages before the indexation launch. Content with only one language can exist as a draft or remain unindexed.
