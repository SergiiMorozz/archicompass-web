# Article 14 notice procedure for professional profiles

## Purpose

This procedure applies when ArchiCompass creates or supplements a professional profile using personal data obtained from a public source rather than directly from the professional.

It does not apply to data submitted directly by a designer or studio through their own ArchiCompass account.

## Before public visibility

Before a profile sourced from public information is made visible, the responsible ArchiCompass team member must record:

1. the profile identifier and the public source URL;
2. the date on which the information was collected;
3. the categories of information used;
4. the purpose and legal basis recorded in the Privacy Policy;
5. the contact channel for the professional; and
6. whether an Article 14 notice was sent or a documented legal exception applies.

Only information necessary for the profile may be copied. Personal contact details that are not needed for the public profile must not be published.

## Notice timing and content

Where no documented exception applies, ArchiCompass sends an Article 14 GDPR notice to the professional:

1. within one month of obtaining the data;
2. no later than the first direct communication with that person; or
3. no later than the first disclosure of the data to another recipient,

whichever occurs first.

The notice must identify ArchiCompass as the controller, explain the source of the data, categories of data, purposes and legal basis, retention approach, relevant data-subject rights including objection, and link to the current Privacy Policy.

## Requests and review

Requests to correct, hide, or delete a sourced profile are prioritised. The requester must be able to contact ArchiCompass at contact@archicompass.pl. The final action and date are recorded with the source record.

## Operational control

No team member may publish a profile sourced from public information until the above record is complete. The first administrative workflow that supports importing or publishing sourced profiles must include these fields and a publication block when the record is incomplete.
