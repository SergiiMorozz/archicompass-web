2. What are cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information saved on the User's device when using a website.

ArchiCompass may also use other browser storage mechanisms, in particular:

* localStorage;
* sessionStorage;
* technical session identifiers;
* authentication tokens;
* similar technologies necessary for the operation of the web application.

In the remainder of this document, the term "cookies" also includes these similar technologies, unless the context indicates otherwise.

⸻

3. Categories of technologies used

3.1. Necessary technologies

Necessary technologies are used to ensure the proper and secure operation of ArchiCompass.

They may be used in particular for:

* logging in and maintaining the User's session;
* authentication;
* Account protection;
* fraud prevention;
* permission control;
* handling file uploads;
* saving settings required for the operation of the Service;
* maintaining form state;
* ensuring the continuity of the Service's functionality.

Such technologies may be used without the User's separate consent to the extent that their use is necessary to provide the service requested by the User or to transmit an electronic communication.

Blocking them in the browser may cause the Service to malfunction.

⸻

3.2. Preferences and interface storage

ArchiCompass may locally save information regarding:

* the selected language;
* interface preferences;
* the state of certain forms;
* the current stage of the AI Project Compass;
* temporary file previews;
* privacy and cookie settings.

These data are used primarily to ensure the continuity and convenience of using the Service.

If a given technology is necessary to perform a function selected by the User, it may be used as a necessary technology.

⸻

3.3. Limited technical statistics

ArchiCompass may use a random technical browser or browser tab identifier to limit the multiple counting of the same Designer Profile view.

This mechanism is not intended to identify the User based on their first name, surname, or email address.

Statistical data may be aggregated or anonymised.

⸻

As of the effective date of this Policy, ArchiCompass does not use optional third-party analytical cookies, unless the User is informed about their implementation and – if required – provides appropriate consent.

If ArchiCompass implements, for example, Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, PostHog, or a similar tool requiring consent in the future, it will only be activated after the User has made the appropriate choice.

As of the effective date of this Policy, ArchiCompass does not use advertising or remarketing cookies and does not use cookies to create advertising profiles of Users.

If such technologies are implemented in the future, this Policy and the consent management mechanism will be updated accordingly before their activation.
