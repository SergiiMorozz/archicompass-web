1. Why we are publishing this information

ArchiCompass uses artificial intelligence solutions as tools to support the User in organising inspiration, preparing a Design Brief, and finding Designers who meet specific needs.

This page explains:

* where ArchiCompass uses AI;
* what data may be analysed;
* what AI does not do;
* how we maintain human oversight;
* what the limitations of the technology are;
* how we protect data;
* how to report issues regarding the operation of AI functions.

ArchiCompass designs and uses AI functions in compliance with relevant legal requirements, including Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 – the AI Act, the GDPR, and consumer protection regulations.

⸻

2. Where we use AI

AI Project Compass

AI may support the analysis of:

* inspiration photos;
* stylistic preferences;
* type of investment;
* nature of the space;
* declared User needs;
* other information provided during the creation of the Brief.

On this basis, AI may assist in preparing, among other things:

* a description of the preferred style;
* interior characteristics;
* a colour palette;
* material directions;
* visual features;
* tips for the Design Brief;
* other information helpful during a conversation with a Designer.

⸻

3. Matching Designers

ArchiCompass may use the data contained in the Brief and other project parameters to indicate Designers who may meet the Client's needs.

Matching may take into account, in particular:

* location;
* scope of the project;
* type of investment;
* budget;
* stylistic preferences;
* information contained in Designers' Profiles and Portfolios;
* area of operation;
* other declared project parameters.

AI may assist in interpreting certain information, e.g., stylistic features resulting from inspiration.

The result of the Matching is of a recommendatory nature.

ArchiCompass does not select a Designer for the Client.

⸻

4. Humans maintain control

The User independently decides:

* whether to launch the AI analysis;
* which photos to upload;
* whether to save the result;
* what information will be included in the Brief;
* whether to send an Enquiry;
* which Designer to approach;
* whether to commence cooperation.

The Designer is independently responsible for their offer, advice, design, and the execution of services.

AI does not enter into a Design Agreement on behalf of the User or the Designer.

⸻

5. How to recognise an AI function

Functions and results directly generated or significantly supported by AI are marked in the interface with appropriate information, e.g.:

"Analysis supported by AI"
or
"Result generated using AI".

If the User is directly using an AI function, ArchiCompass informs them of this in a visible manner in the context of the given functionality.

The AI Act requires informing a person of direct interaction with AI in appropriate cases.

⸻

6. Information before photo analysis

Reference photos are not automatically sent to the AI model.

The transfer takes place only after the User consciously launches the analysis function.

Before starting the analysis, the User receives information that the selected materials will be transferred to an external AI technology provider.

In a single request, AI Project Compass currently analyses a maximum of six photos.

⸻

7. What information may be transferred to AI

Depending on the functionality used, this may include:

* selected photos;
* type of investment;
* selected stylistic direction;
* visual features;
* information regarding the project;
* other data necessary to prepare the result.

ArchiCompass does not transfer login data or phone numbers as part of the standard AI Project Compass photo analysis.

The User should not upload:

* identity documents;
* payment card data;
* confidential documents;
* special category data;
* photos of people, if their use is not necessary or the User does not have a basis for their processing.

⸻

8. AI technology providers

ArchiCompass may integrate AI models provided by external providers.

The currently used provider or configuration may change as technology develops.

Information regarding current providers, the processing of personal data, and any potential data transfers is described in the Privacy Policy.

ArchiCompass does not use Users' private photos as a public content database.

⸻

9. AI limitations

AI systems use statistical models and may:

* misinterpret a photo;
* fail to recognise an important element;
* generate incomplete information;
* propose a solution that is impossible to execute;
* present a result that does not correspond to all of the User's preferences;
* generate similar results for different Users.

The AI result should be treated as a starting point for further work, and not as a guaranteed professional assessment.

⸻

10. AI does not replace a professional

AI Project Compass does not constitute:

* an architectural design;
* an executive design;
* technical documentation;
* structural expertise;
* a cost estimate;
* legal advice;
* tax advice;
* financial advice;
* an assessment of compliance with building regulations;
* a guarantee of investment feasibility.

Before implementing a project, important information should be verified with an appropriate specialist.

⸻

11. No automated decisions with significant effects

ArchiCompass does not use AI Project Compass or the Matching mechanism to make decisions towards the User that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect them solely through automated processing.

Final decisions belong to the Users.

⸻

12. Prohibited and undesirable uses

ArchiCompass does not design AI Project Compass for the purpose of:

* recognising protected characteristics of a person based on appearance;
* assessing the value or credibility of a human;
* social scoring;
* manipulating the User;
* exploiting the vulnerabilities of children or particularly vulnerable persons;
* biometric identification of persons;
* drawing conclusions regarding health, sexual orientation, political views, or other special characteristics of a person.

The analysis is intended to concern interiors, inspiration, and design needs.

⸻

13. Security and monitoring

ArchiCompass may monitor:

* reported errors;
* unexpected results;
* security issues;
* changes in performance quality;
* the impact of model or prompt updates.

If necessary, we may:

* change system instructions;
* restrict a function;
* change a model or provider;
* add security measures;
* temporarily disable a function.

14. Reporting issues

If an AI result:

* is evidently incorrect;
* contains inappropriate content;
* appears discriminatory;
* violates privacy;
